# Contributing to DFC Taxonomies

👍🎉 First off, thanks for taking the time to contribute! 🎉👍

This is a set of guidelines for contributing changes and enhancements to the SKOS taxonomies files used within the DFC Standard.

### Table of Contents

## Code of conduct
This project and all participants are goverened by the DFC Code of Conduct[^1].

## What should I know before I get started?

### How is the Standard structured
The DFC Standard has about 10 active repositories.

For details of the various repositories and their purpose, see our [readme](https://github.com/datafoodconsortium?view_as=public#standard).

## How Can I Contribute?

We'd love you to contribute to help the standard become even better. Here are a few ways we can think of - if there are others, please reach out to us on [Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/datafoodconsortium/shared_invite/zt-2xviz184m-pxxroYnx620LM19fcHhPmg).

### Reporting Bugs

Bugs should be reported against the relevant repository. Please us the appropriate issue template and provide as much information as possible to assist the team in responding to your report.

### Suggesting Enhancements

If you feel something is missing from the taxonomies, you can either suggest it in [an issue](https://github.com/datafoodconsortium/taxonomies/issues/new?template=feature_request.md), or get involved in directly updating the taxonomies using our VOCbench server (see below for details).

### Contributing to the Standard

We welcome community input, in particulat around enriching our taxonomies. If you'd like to get involved in that, please register an account on our [VOCbench server](https://vocbench.dfc-standard.org/vocbench3/#/Registration/0). Than [contact us on Slack](https://slack.com/app_redirect?channel=C020KLNTDF0) and we'll make sure you've got appropriate access to edit & create concepts within the appropriate SKOS vocabularies.
